About me

More photographs are still to come

I grew up in Lucknow, and it's hard to think of a better city in India to be a kid in. Awadhi through and through: chikankari embroidery in the old quarters, and food taken seriously enough that UNESCO put the city on its Creative Cities list for gastronomy in 2025.

My school there is the largest in the world by enrolment, which mostly meant exchange trips and a passport stamp long before my friends had one. I haven't stopped moving since. A Swedish company, a couple of years in Spain, and a company of my own somewhere in the middle that taught me more than any job has.

Toastmasters did more for my career than any job did. I joined with a real fear of speaking to a room, and a year of it, then a year running the club, sorted that out. It changed how I write too. Hold a room for seven minutes with nothing to hide behind and you stop padding.

Which led to the hardest thing I've done. The first official TEDx in my district, on my licence, organised on my own, in a city with no airport. It ended up the first from the district to make the official TEDx channel.

I cook most nights. It started as a way to avoid studying and turned into how I settle into a new country. I chase dishes rather than recipes, mostly out of Falastin and Flavour, and I keep Marco Pierre White's rule that presentation matters but taste matters more. It's all on Utsav.

Otherwise there's a guitar, which I play for no reason other than I enjoy it, and travel whenever I can. Mountains over beaches, every time. Best trip so far was southern Germany with a friend who grew up there and knew exactly where to stop.
