About me

I run growth for a living: paid acquisition, retention, and the measurement underneath both. Over five years that has taken me through a supplement brand in India, Sweden's largest eCommerce platform, a biotechnology startup based in Cambridge, and an MBA at ESADE.

The rest of the time I build my own things, and six of them are on the homepage. Most of them began because I wanted something that did not exist. Utsav is a recipe site because I cook most nights and wanted the recipes written the way I actually make them. tvbadger is a review site because I watch a lot and a star rating was never a useful answer.

I end up organising things as well. I held the licence for the first official TEDx in my district and ran it end to end, I ran a free quiz competition across Indian campuses, and I was president of a Toastmasters club for a year.
